**THE TEAM**

Born from X, the moonshot factory, and working alongside teams at Google, we’re building a new type of robot. One that can learn by itself, to help anyone with (almost) anything. With one foot in today, and the other in tomorrow, we seek to turn the helper robots of science fiction novels into reality. Doing this requires taking on the hardest problems in robotics. All so we can work towards a future where helper robots are a part of a better everyday, for everyone.

We know that no company, no moonshot, no wild idea is greater than the efforts of the people behind it. Our team is thoughtful, driven and deliberately diverse, working together in a creative and collaborative environment, where everyone’s perspectives and expertise is valued.

We’re looking for an HRI PRODUCT MANAGEMENT INTERN FOR SUMMER 2023 that will bring their creative experience and unique perspective to the way our robots interact with humans.

**WHAT YOU WILL LEARN**

*   Lots about robots and how they work

*   How to create human-robot experiences from 0 to 1

*   Fundamental product management skills

*   Creative prototyping skills

**RESPONSIBILITIES**

*   Partner with UXR to understand human needs and uncover HRI opportunities

*   Define a new and impactful HRI feature, tool, or process (feature requirements, flow charts, interactive prototypes, etc)

*   Lead a cross functional team to prototype new interactions designed to solve real problems

*   Champion the team’s work by communicating with and presenting to stakeholders

**MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS**

*   Currently enrolled in an academic program and working towards a Bachelors, Masters, or PhD

*   Deep interest in robots

*   Endlessly curious — a lifelong learner

*   Empathetic and creative

*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills

*   Strong initiative and a bias to action

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

*   Background in Robotics, HCI, Design, Gaming, Animation, AR/VR, Sound Design, Social Psychology, or other relevant fields
