Digit

Access Spec Sheet

→

Deploy Digit

→

Designed to excel in spaces where people already work, Digit’s human-centric form factor means that manufacturing floors and warehouse spaces don’t have to be redesigned. It’s a solution that’s ready to help today.

Adaptable

Digit can walk into existing facilities and address the hardest-to-automate portions of your workflows. With interchangeable end effectors and AI-powered learning, it can evolve to changing needs. 

Durable

Digit has a 35 pound carrying capacity, 4 hour battery life, and the capability to work continuous shifts. It’s designed to endure strenuous conditions on the most difficult assembly lines. 

Trustworthy

None of Digit’s capabilities would matter if it wasn’t safe to be in your facility. That’s why our team worked with industry experts to develop safe and cooperative behaviors required for deployment at scale.

Arc

Deploy Digit

→

Arc distills humanoid robotics from complex technology into practical, accessible tools that operations teams can deploy and manage with confidence. As the cloud-based platform powering every Digit deployment, Arc eliminates traditional barriers to adoption through intuitive design, rapid integration, and enterprise-grade reliability.  

Intuitive

Arc provides a centralized command center for your automated workforce. Real-time fleet management gives you complete visibility into operations. Monitor robot status, track productivity metrics, manage task assignments, and optimize workflows.  

Integrated

Connectivity to existing warehouse management, execution, and manufacturing systems is seamless. Arc deploys quickly alongside Digit, getting your humanoid workforce operational with minimal disruption.

Secure

Cloud-native architecture provides industry-leading security through encrypted comms, single sign-on, and role based access controls. Arc is designed to protect your data, while enabling the flexibility of cloud deployment.
