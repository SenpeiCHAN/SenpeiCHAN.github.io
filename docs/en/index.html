Approach

How I move a project forward

This is a common path. In practice, a project moves back and forth between research, prototyping, and testing.

  1. 01

    Literature & cases

    I review literature, prior cases, common analytical frameworks, and visual references to understand what is already known and where existing solutions stop.

  2. 02

    Context & experience

    I enter the real setting, observe tasks, environments, and constraints, and experience how the problem occurs firsthand.

  3. 03

    Interviews & analysis

    Interviews help explain why; surveys show distributions and differences. I then use suitable descriptive statistics and cross-comparisons.

  4. 04

    Insights & questions

    I organize patterns in the material, separate evidence from assumptions, and frame questions that design or an experiment can answer.

  5. 05

    Concepts & prototypes

    I choose sketches, interaction flows, structural models, code, or hardware at the fidelity needed to answer the question.

  6. 06

    Testing & feedback

    Target users try the prototype in concrete tasks while I record behavior, errors, task completion, and subjective feedback.

  7. 07

    Refinement & conclusion

    I revise and test again until the work is ready to deliver. If the hypothesis fails, I document the conclusion and its limits.

Research focus

Questions I’m exploring

  1. 01

    Human-smart hardware

    I look at how movement, touch, and physical feedback make a device easier to understand. Instead of placing every control on a screen, I ask whether turning, tapping, holding, or moving can become clear inputs.

  2. 02

    Human-AI

    I care about how people see, understand, and correct AI. Rather than hiding the model, I want recognition results, errors, and system feedback to remain perceptible.

  3. 03

    How research shapes smart hardware

    User research should do more than define features. Observed actions, difficulties, and task order can also shape form, internal mechanisms, and system feedback.
