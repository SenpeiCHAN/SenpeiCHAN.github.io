# Human-Robot Interaction / 人机机器人交互
## Definition
研究人与机器人在物理、认知、社会和组织层面的相互作用。
## Key Concepts
HRI, role allocation, trust, safety, cooperation
## Related Disciplines
Robotics; HCI; Human Factors; AI; Design Research
## Important Questions
人如何理解机器人能力和边界？机器人何时应主动、何时应等待？
## Typical Methods
literature review; controlled experiment; field study; Wizard-of-Oz
## Design Relevance
把机器人从工程对象转成可共同工作的交互对象。
## Application Scenarios
服务、医疗、工厂、公共空间
## Related Knowledge Graph Nodes
discipline_hri; concept_robot_autonomy; metric_trust
